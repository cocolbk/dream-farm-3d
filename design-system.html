6 · 3D visual language

Textured low-poly diorama: grass/soil/wood/roof canvas textures, glossy produce and glass, detailed crops (maize stalks with husks, staked tomatoes, carrot mounds, wheat heads, ribbed pumpkins) and buildings (barn gambrel roof + X-brace door, shop awning + sign, coops with runs, garages with tire stacks). Hemisphere + key sun with shadows + cool fill light, fog. Plot states always carry a text label, never color-only.
